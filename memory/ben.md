## Recurring themes
- Big IPOs (SpaceX, Anthropic, later OpenAI): surge, then ~50% drop, drain liquidity from crypto; 21st of month has marked BTC local tops since July 2026.
- 200-week MA is not a guaranteed floor (2015, 2020 went ~30% below); whales usually appear at Q4 lows (absent so far, 14 Nov); capitulation = one bounce then final drop.
- Midterm-year Q4 weakness; February / summer / Q4 low pattern across assets.
- Commodities (gold, silver) tend to bottom before BTC in midterm years.
- 2018 and 2022 analogues; first-hike seasonality; hawkish dollar drains liquidity.
- On-chain metrics must reset before a bull market starts ("bull's dilemma"; "altcoiner's dilemma").
