1. Thesis and stance

Ben applies his SpaceX IPO framework to Anthropic. His point is that the IPO date matters for other markets: liquidity leaves crypto into mega-IPOs, then equities and Bitcoin tend to give back the launch rally. Tone is consistent with the 18 Nov video (cautiously bearish into a Q4 low around the December FOMC), with the IPO supplying a more specific calendar. He repeatedly hedges: "who knows, maybe the market will surprise us". He also notes personally that he bought SpaceX (explicitly not financial advice).

5. Reasoning chains

  1. IPOs typically spike on launch, often extending for a couple of days because early sellers get squeezed out of their short views, then dump; the post-dump low tends to be more viable.
  2. Evidence: SpaceX (+50% then -53%), Tesla 2010 (+73% then -50%, macro low, then ~3 years before price discovery), Coinbase 2021 (initial wick, low ~a month later, rally, then much lower), Rivian (~50% drop, rally, further dump), Apple 1980 (~40% drop, rally, then a recession and a lower low in 1982).
  3. Large IPOs absorb liquidity, so BTC weakens around them (ETF outflows).
  4. SpaceX and Anthropic IPO path of BTC look "almost a carbon copy": new local low going in, bounce, then a lower low ~18-20 days later, which lands on 9 Dec FOMC.
  5. 2026 Nasdaq YTD path resembles 2018: drop, rally, larger drop that became the low (25 Dec 2018).
